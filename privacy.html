BookIt Privacy Policy

BookIt: Wrestling Booking Sim · Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version: your game stays on your device. We don't show ads, we don't sell data and we don't use outside tracking tools. Besides a few anonymous play stats you can turn off, only what you choose to post to Community, back up to the cloud or send as feedback leaves your device.

Who we are

BookIt is an independent, fan-made game. In this policy, "we" means the developer of BookIt. You can reach us at bookitsimgame@gmail.com. This policy covers the game on the web and the BookIt app on Google Play, which runs the same game.

On your device

Your saves, notes, settings, wrestler library and private photos are stored on your device only. Nobody else can see them, including us. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes them, so use Back up or Cloud saves from the Menu if you want a copy.

What we collect, and why

DataWhenWhy
A random account idWhen you first open Community or Cloud savesTo keep your posts and cloud saves yours. We don't ask for your name.
Email address (optional)Only if you add it to keep your accountTo send you sign-in links so you can use your account on another device. Other players never see it.
Community posts and profileWhen you post or edit your profileShown to everyone who plays: rosters, saves, photos you share, your display name, bio, color, likes and follows.
Cloud savesWhen you upload a save or turn on auto backupSo you can load your game on another device. Only you can read them.
Feedback and reportsWhen you send feedback, report a post or the game crashesTo fix bugs and moderate Community. They include your message, the game version, your week number, your browser type and, for crashes, the error message. Only the developer can read them.
Anonymous play statsWhile you play, unless you turn them off in Menu → SettingsTo see how many people play and what to improve: when you open the game, how long you play, shows you run and their rating, companies you start, tutorial steps, crashes, which screens you open, the game version, and whether you play in a browser, an installed app or the Google Play app. Tied to a random id made on your device, never to your account, email or name. IP addresses aren't stored with them. Only the developer can see them, and they're deleted after 400 days. They start off if your browser sends Global Privacy Control.
Supporter status (web only)If you buy BookIt Supporter on the websiteWhether your plan is active, when it renews, and Stripe's customer and subscription ids. Card details go to Stripe and never reach the game.

We don't collect your location, contacts, phone number, advertising id or device identifiers, and we don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores Community, cloud saves, accounts, feedback and play stats, encrypted in transit.
  • Stripe processes BookIt Supporter payments made on the website. The Google Play app doesn't sell anything.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game, and Google Fonts serves its fonts. Like any website, they receive your IP address when they send you files.

How long we keep it

Community posts and cloud saves stay until you delete them. Feedback and reports are kept only as long as they're useful for fixing bugs and moderating. Play stats are deleted after 400 days. Supporter records are kept while your plan is active and as long as payment records must be kept by law.

Deleting your data and your account

You can delete your cloud saves from Menu → Cloud saves and your Community posts from Community → My profile at any time.

To delete your whole account, email bookitsimgame@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my BookIt account". Include the email address you signed in with, or your Community display name if you never added an email. We'll delete your account, profile, posts, likes, follows, cloud saves and email address within 30 days and reply to confirm. Payment records Stripe must keep by law are not affected.

Children

BookIt is meant for players aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has posted to Community or created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Community house rules

Share only what you made or are allowed to share. No slurs, harassment, spam or personal details, and no real photos of people without permission. Player-made content about real people or companies is unofficial fan work, and names belong to their owners. We can remove any post or profile that breaks the rules.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be shown in the game's What's new.
